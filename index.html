Our Services

Comprehensive dental care
under one roof

From routine check-ups to advanced restorations, everything your family needs, under one roof in BTM Layout.

Speciality

Root Canal Treatment

Advanced rotary endodontics saves infected or damaged teeth with precision, efficiency, and structural integrity. Using modern specialized instruments, this treatment thoroughly cleans, seals, and preserves your natural tooth structure.

  • Single and multi-rooted teeth, anteriors & posteriors
  • Hand and advanced rotary endodontic techniques
  • Re-treatment of failed root canals
Restorative

Crowns & Bridges

Ceramic and zirconia crowns and bridges that restore strength, function, and natural aesthetics. Precision-fabricated to match your natural tooth shade and bite.

  • Metal-free ceramic & zirconia crowns
  • Fixed bridges for missing teeth
  • Post-RCT crown placement
Advanced

Dental Implants

The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Titanium implants fuse with the jawbone to create a permanent, stable foundation for crowns that look and function like natural teeth.

  • Single-tooth & multiple implants
  • Implant-supported dentures
  • Bone grafting where required
Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade whitening that delivers visibly brighter results safely. More effective and longer-lasting than over-the-counter products.

  • In-clinic power whitening
  • Custom take-home trays
  • Safe for sensitive teeth options
Orthodontics

Orthodontics

Straighten your teeth and correct bite issues with traditional braces or modern clear aligner options. Suitable for teens and adults.

  • Metal & ceramic braces
  • Clear aligners / invisible braces
  • Retainer fabrication & fitting
Cosmetic

Cosmetic Dentistry

Complete smile makeovers using veneers, bonding, contouring, and other aesthetic treatments. Subtle enhancements or transformative results, designed around you.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Composite bonding
  • Gum contouring & smile design
Restorative

Fillings & Restorations

Tooth-coloured composite and ceramic fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. Strong, durable, and completely invisible.

  • Composite (tooth-coloured) fillings
  • Ceramic inlays & onlays
  • Replacement of old amalgam fillings
Preventive

Cleaning & Prevention

Regular professional cleaning is the foundation of long-term oral health. Our comprehensive hygiene appointments go well beyond a surface clean.

  • Scaling & root planing
  • Professional polishing
  • Fluoride treatment & sealants
Surgical

Tooth Extractions

Simple and surgical extractions performed with precision and care, minimising trauma and discomfort. Wisdom tooth removal included.

  • Simple & surgical extractions
  • Wisdom tooth removal
  • Post-extraction care guidance
Preventive

Paediatric Dentistry

Child-friendly dental care that builds healthy habits for life. We make first dental visits positive, comfortable, and fun for young patients.

  • Child check-ups & cleanings
  • Milk tooth treatment & extractions
  • Pit & fissure sealants
Restorative

Dentures & Prosthetics

Full and partial dentures crafted for comfort, function, and a natural appearance. Removable and fixed options available.

  • Complete & partial dentures
  • Flexible & metal-frame dentures
  • Denture relines & repairs
General

General Dentistry

Your premium entry point to complete oral health. Thorough check-ups, precise diagnosis using digital X-rays, and professional deep cleaning to keep your smile healthy for life.

  • Comprehensive oral examination & OPG X-rays
  • Personalised treatment planning
  • Professional scaling, polishing & fluoride treatment
  • Preventive care & oral hygiene guidance
Periodontal

Gum Disease Care

Diagnosis and comprehensive treatment of gum disease, from gingivitis to advanced periodontitis, to protect your oral health long-term.

  • Gingivitis and periodontitis treatment
  • Deep scaling and curettage
  • Gum health maintenance plans
